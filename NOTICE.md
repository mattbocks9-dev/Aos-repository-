## No warranty, not an official rules source

The app is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind (see `LICENSE`). Data may be incomplete, out of
date or wrong. The NPC captain's matchup estimates are heuristic and are only for practice. Always use the
official Games Workshop publications and your event's rules pack for actual play.

## Privacy

The app has no accounts, no analytics and no server. Teams, lists and any images you add are saved only in
your own browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). Loading the site fetches web fonts from Google Fonts and
battleplan map pictures from Wahapedia, which may receive your IP address as part of those requests.
